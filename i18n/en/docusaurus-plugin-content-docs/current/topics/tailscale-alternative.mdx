# Can Larktun Be a Tailscale Alternative?

If you want to connect distributed servers, home NAS boxes, routers, and mobile devices into a zero-trust WireGuard mesh, **Tailscale** is a proven benchmark. **Larktun** builds on the same WireGuard and Headscale networking foundation while addressing practical gaps faced by individual users and teams who want managed multi-tenancy, China-friendly relay performance, native Chinese/Unicode device names, and no-VPN mobile remote tools.

In many everyday and production scenarios, Larktun works directly as a drop-in Tailscale alternative—and because it remains compatible with standard Tailscale clients, you can even test or migrate incrementally without rebuilding your devices.

## Side-by-Side Comparison: Larktun vs. Tailscale vs. Self-Hosted Headscale

| Capability | Larktun (Managed SaaS) | Tailscale (Official SaaS) | Self-Hosted Headscale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control-plane operations | Fully managed SaaS | Fully managed SaaS | Self-hosted server, DB, TLS & upgrades |
| Multi-tenant isolation | Native per-tenant tailnet, ACL, routes & DNS | Tailnet per org / account | Single tailnet by default |
| China / cross-ISP relay options | Built-in domestic relays + VIP + user-managed DERP | Global DERP (often higher latency in China) | Manual self-hosted DERP setup |
| Device naming & localization | Native Chinese / Unicode aliases & bilingual UI | ASCII hostname focus | ASCII hostname focus |
| Mobile access without system VPN | Built-in `tsnet` app (SSH, SFTP, Web, RDP, WebRTC) | System VPN client required | Relies on Tailscale system VPN client |
| Tailscale client compatibility | Compatible via `--login-server=https://hs.larktun.com` | Native | Compatible via `--login-server` |
| Built-in remote desktop & AI tools | Integrated RDP, WebRTC remote control & AI assistant | Network transport only | Network transport only |

## When Larktun Is an Especially Strong Fit

- **Users needing reliable China & cross-region relays**: Choose between free shared relays, dedicated VIP relays, or your own self-hosted DERP nodes directly from the console.
- **Home lab & NAS remote access without port forwarding**: Connect Synology, ASUSTOR, Linux NAS, OpenWrt routers, and home PCs under a free personal tenant.
- **Mobile ops without fighting system VPN slots**: Use SSH, SFTP, internal Web browsing, and RDP/WebRTC from iPhone, iPad, Android phones, tablets, or Android TV while keeping your corporate VPN active.
- **Teams who want Headscale's open-source roots without running ops**: Skip maintaining reverse proxies, PostgreSQL backups, TLS renewals, and custom DERP clusters yourself.
- **Multi-customer or multi-department governance**: Isolate each project or customer into its own tenant with independent IP ranges, ACLs, and audit trails.

## Smooth Coexistence and Easy Trial

You do not need an all-or-nothing migration to evaluate Larktun:

1. **Reuse existing Tailscale clients**: On platforms like Synology DSM, ASUSTOR, or iOS where the official Tailscale client is already installed, point it to Larktun using `--login-server=https://hs.larktun.com` (see [Log in with Tailscale Client](../getting-started/tailscale-client-login)).
2. **Use Larktun's mobile app with your existing network**: The Larktun iOS and Android apps also support third-party auth-key login directly into your existing Tailscale or self-hosted Headscale network (see [iOS Third-Party Login](../getting-started/ios-third-party-login)).

## When Tailscale or Self-Hosted Headscale May Still Be Better

- Stay with **Tailscale** if your organization already standardizes on Tailscale Enterprise features, global identity integrations, and your users have consistently low-latency access to Tailscale's global DERP network.
- Choose **self-hosted Headscale** if internal security policy strictly forbids any external SaaS control plane and your team has the engineering capacity to operate and secure the control plane and DERP fleet in-house.
